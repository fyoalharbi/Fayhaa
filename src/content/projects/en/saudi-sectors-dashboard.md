## What you can see

1. The number of sectors
2. The number of agencies
3. A slicer to sort agencies by sector
4. A slicer to sort agencies by type of agency
5. A list of the agencies
6. A description of the selected agency

## How it was built

Built end to end during my internship at Hackathonat with Reem: the data was scraped from the web, cleaned, and visualized in Power BI.
